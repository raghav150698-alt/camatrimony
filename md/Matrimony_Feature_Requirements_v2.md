# Matrimony Platform: Feature Requirements Document (FRD)

## 1. Authentication & Onboarding
* **Primary Registration & Verification:** Google Signup acts as the primary registration method, with mandatory mobile number verification at the initial stage.
* **Account Manager Specification:** "Profile Created By" dropdown to specify who is managing the account (options: Self, Parent, Sibling, Relative, Friend). 

## 2. Profile Management
* **Basic Information:** Age, Height, Weight, Marital Status (Never Married, Divorced, Widow/Widower), and Diet (Vegetarian, Non-Veg, Eggetarian).
* **Cultural & Religious Details:** Religion, Caste, Sub-caste, and Gothram.
* **Astrological Details:** Time, Date, and Place of Birth, Rasi (Moon Sign), Nakshatram (Star), and Dosham/Kuja Dosham status.
* **Education & Career:** Highest Degree, Employed In (Govt, Private, Business), Occupation, Annual Income, and Current Work Location (including NRI statuses).
* **Family Details:** Family Status (Middle class, Upper-middle class, Rich), Family Type (Joint, Nuclear), Father's/Mother's Occupation, and Number of Siblings.
* **Partner Preferences:** Strict filters for what the user is looking for in a partner (Age range, Caste, Education, Income).
* **Media Gallery:** Upload photos (with automatic watermarking to prevent misuse) and short video bios.

## 3. Search, Discovery & Engagement
* **Profile ID Search:** Quickly look up a specific profile shared via word-of-mouth using a unique ID.
* **Express Interest:** Send a standard interest request with Accept/Decline functionality.
* **Shortlisting:** Bookmark or "Star" profiles to save and review later with family members.
* **In-App Chat:** Secure messaging system that unlocks only after mutual acceptance or for users with premium memberships.
* **Push Notifications & Alerts:** Real-time updates for received interests, matches, and chat messages via email.

## 4. Privacy, Security & Trust
* **Profile Verification:** Trust badges for verified profiles via Government ID ([Aadhaar Redacted], Passport) or live selfie checks.
* **Photo Privacy Controls:** Options to set photo visibility to "Visible to All," "Visible only to Accepted Matches," or "Password Protected."
* **Contact Detail Privacy:** Hide email and phone numbers from free members; only allow premium members or mutually accepted interests to view them.
* **Block & Report:** User tools to report fake profiles or block abusive users.

## 5. Monetization & Memberships
* **Tiered Subscription Plans:** Configurable premium membership tiers: **Gold**, **Diamond**, and **Platinum**.
* **Plan + Mediation Services (Add-on):** A premium assisted service where a dedicated team member handles all communication, doubts, and questions between the two parties. 
  * **3 Months:** ₹500 extra
  * **6 Months:** ₹1000 extra
  * **12 Months:** ₹1500 extra
* **Payment Gateway Integration:** Razorpay integration for seamless and secure transaction processing.
* **Premium Perks:** Enhanced features such as the ability to view phone numbers (with a set limit, e.g., 50 contacts per cycle).

## 6. Admin Control Panel & CMS
* **User Management:** Comprehensive tools to approve, edit, suspend, or ban user profiles.
* **KYC Approval Dashboard:** Dedicated portal to manually verify uploaded government IDs and grant "Verified" badges to users.
* **Subscription Management:** Create new subscription plans, apply discount promo codes, and track user payments/revenue.
* **Analytics Dashboard:** Track platform health metrics including daily active users, revenue, male-to-female ratio, and total successful matches.
* **Staff Roles & Permissions:** Ability to add customer support agents with restricted/role-based dashboard access.
* **Content Management System (CMS) & Global Settings:**
  * **Dynamic Page Creation:** Build and manage essential legal pages such as the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms & Conditions**.
  * **Custom Pages:** Add, edit, or delete additional informative pages (e.g., About Us, Success Stories, FAQ, Safety Guidelines).
  * **Site Settings:** Centrally manage core website details, including the official support email, physical address, and contact phone numbers displayed globally across the footer and contact pages.
